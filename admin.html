Step 1 of 3

Who is the renter?

Shown on receipts and messages. Use the name on the rent agreement. The renter signs in with this number. Login details and receipts are sent to it.

Step 2 of 3

Room and rent date

Which room or flat the renter stays in. Choose Other if it is not listed. The day of every month by which rent should be paid. After this day the renter shows as overdue. Rent is counted from this month. Earlier months will never show as due.

Step 3 of 3

Rent and password

The amount the renter pays every month. The renter signs in with the phone number and this password. You can send it to them on the next screen.
